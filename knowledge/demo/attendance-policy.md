# DEMO SmartHR HR Policy - Attendance Policy

> **DEMO DOCUMENT.** Written to test SmartHR's document search. This is not a
> real company policy and must not be relied on. Replace with your own policy
> before using SmartHR in production.

Document owner: People Operations (demo)
Version: 1.0 (demo)

## 1. Working hours

Standard working hours are **09:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Thursday**, totalling 40
hours per week, including a one-hour unpaid lunch break.

## 2. Flexible start

Employees may start any time between **08:00 and 10:00** and finish the
corresponding eight hours later, provided they are present during the core
hours of **11:00 to 15:00**.

## 3. Recording attendance

Employees record a check-in on arrival and a check-out on leaving, through
SmartHR. Attendance is recorded on the day it happens; a missed check-in must
be corrected by the employee's manager, who records the correction in SmartHR.

## 4. Lateness

Arrival after the agreed start time is recorded as **Late**.

- Up to 15 minutes late: no action, but it is recorded.
- More than 15 minutes: the employee informs their manager the same morning.
- Three or more late arrivals in one calendar month: the manager holds a
  documented conversation with the employee.

Repeated lateness beyond that is handled under the disciplinary procedure.

## 5. Absence without notice

An employee who is absent without notifying their manager is recorded as
**Absent**, and the day is unpaid. Absence without notice for **three
consecutive working days** is treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## 6. Overtime

Overtime must be approved by a manager in advance. Approved overtime is
compensated as time off in lieu, taken within 30 days.
