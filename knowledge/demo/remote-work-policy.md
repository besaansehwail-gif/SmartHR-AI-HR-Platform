# DEMO SmartHR HR Policy - Remote Work Policy

> **DEMO DOCUMENT.** Written to test SmartHR's document search. This is not a
> real company policy and must not be relied on. Replace with your own policy
> before using SmartHR in production.

Document owner: People Operations (demo)
Version: 1.0 (demo)

## 1. Hybrid working

Employees may work remotely for up to **two days per week**. The remaining
three days are worked from the office.

Which days are remote is agreed with the direct manager and kept stable, so
teams know when colleagues are available in person.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees who have completed **three months** of
service and whose role does not require being on site. Roles in reception,
facilities and IT hardware support are on site full time.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement is exceptional. It requires approval from the
department head and People Operations, and is reviewed every six months.

## 4. Expectations while working remotely

- Be reachable during core hours, **11:00 to 15:00**.
- Attend scheduled meetings by video with the camera on where practical.
- Record attendance in SmartHR exactly as on an office day.
- Work from a location with a reliable internet connection suitable for calls.

Remote work is not a substitute for childcare or other dependent care.

## 5. Equipment and expenses

The company provides a laptop. Employees working remotely may claim a one-off
allowance of **150 JOD** towards a desk chair or monitor, once every two years,
approved by their manager.

Home internet costs are not reimbursed.

## 6. Working from another country

Working from outside the country requires prior written approval from People
Operations, because of tax and legal implications. Approval is normally limited
to **10 working days** per calendar year.

## 7. Withdrawal

A manager may suspend a remote arrangement, with two weeks' notice, where
business needs or performance require it.
