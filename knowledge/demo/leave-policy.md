# DEMO SmartHR HR Policy - Leave Policy

> **DEMO DOCUMENT.** Written to test SmartHR's document search. This is not a
> real company policy and must not be relied on. Replace with your own policy
> before using SmartHR in production.

Document owner: People Operations (demo)
Version: 1.0 (demo)

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **21 working days** of paid annual leave
per calendar year. Entitlement is earned monthly at 1.75 days per completed
month of service.

Employees in their first year may take annual leave only after completing
three months of continuous service.

Up to **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried over into the following
year. They must be used before 31 March or they lapse.

## 2. Requesting leave

All leave must be requested through SmartHR and approved by the employee's
direct manager before the leave begins.

- Requests of 1 to 3 days: submit at least **3 working days** in advance.
- Requests of 4 days or more: submit at least **10 working days** in advance.

A manager should respond to a request within 3 working days. If a request is
rejected, the manager records the reason in SmartHR.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive **14 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year.

A medical certificate is required for any sick absence of **3 consecutive days
or more**. Sick leave is not deducted from annual leave and cannot be carried
over into the next year.

## 4. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be granted at the company's discretion once annual leave for
the year is exhausted. It requires approval from both the direct manager and
People Operations, and is normally limited to 30 days in a rolling 12 months.

## 5. Public holidays

Public holidays are additional to annual leave. An employee required to work on
a public holiday receives a compensatory day off within 30 days.

## 6. Leave during notice period

Annual leave may not normally be taken during a notice period. Untaken
entitlement is settled in the final salary payment instead.
