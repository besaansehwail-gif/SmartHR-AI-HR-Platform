# DEMO SmartHR HR Policy - Employee Onboarding Policy

> **DEMO DOCUMENT.** Written to test SmartHR's document search. This is not a
> real company policy and must not be relied on. Replace with your own policy
> before using SmartHR in production.

Document owner: People Operations (demo)
Version: 1.0 (demo)

## 1. Before the first day

People Operations completes the following before a new employee arrives:

- Signed contract returned and filed.
- Employee record created in SmartHR, with department, position and manager.
- A SmartHR user account created and linked to the employee record.
- Laptop and accounts requested from IT at least **five working days** ahead.
- The direct manager nominates a **buddy** from the team.

## 2. First day

- The manager welcomes the new employee and introduces the team.
- IT hands over the laptop and completes account setup.
- People Operations runs an induction session covering working hours, leave,
  attendance and the code of conduct.
- The employee confirms their personal details in SmartHR.

## 3. First week

- The manager agrees objectives for the probation period and records them.
- The buddy is available daily for questions.
- The employee completes mandatory training assigned in SmartHR: information
  security awareness, and health and safety.

## 4. First 30 days

- A check-in meeting with the manager at the end of week two.
- A check-in with People Operations at the end of week four.
- Any outstanding training assignments are completed.

## 5. Probation

The probation period is **three months**. A probation review is held in the
final two weeks and the outcome - confirmation or extension - is recorded in
SmartHR by the manager.

Probation may be extended once, by up to three months.

## 6. Responsibilities

| Who | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| People Operations | Contract, records, induction, probation tracking |
| Direct manager | Objectives, buddy, check-ins, probation outcome |
| IT | Laptop, accounts, access |
| Buddy | Day-to-day questions in the first month |
